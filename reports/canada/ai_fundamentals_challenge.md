# AI Fundamentals Challenge — CAIO 2025 National Qualifier

**Problem domain:** Mathematical reasoning, Python/code tracing, algorithmic thinking, and machine-learning fundamentals  
**Evaluation metric:** 100 marks (25 multiple-choice × 2 marks, 10 fill-in-the-blank × 5 marks)  
**Time limit:** 90 minutes

The organizer describes the format and topics on its [Prepare page](https://iaiocanada.com/prepare/). The [official question set](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1g_pzms3KDcsFf_acDQHyYtycpG46oUxQ/view?usp=sharing) is a 22-page PDF.
